# LAST CALL — CASE 002: "THE LAST ROUND"

*A complete, production-ready detective case design document.*

---

## A. CASE OVERVIEW

**Title:** The Last Round

**Premise:** On the night of a private "farewell tasting" event, the owner and head mixologist of an acclaimed rooftop cocktail bar is found dead in the venue's wine cellar, apparently the victim of an intruder who came for a rare bottle. But nothing about the scene quite adds up — and every regular in the room that night was hiding something from her, and from each other.

**Setting:** *Meridian*, a 30th-floor cocktail bar atop the Aldwyn Hotel, occupying two connected floors: the public bar and rooftop deck (Floor 30) and the kitchen, dry storage, wine cellar, and loading dock (Floor 29), linked by a public staircase, a guest elevator, and a camera-free interior service stairwell.

**Victim:** Elena Voss, 42 — owner of Meridian. Publicly a self-made success story. Privately, "Elena Voss" is a reinvented identity: ten years ago, as *Elena Kowalski*, she was the silent architect of an embezzlement scheme at a restaurant group in another city — a scheme her business partner, Marcus Webb, took the full legal fall for. She resurfaced under a new name with the backing of a silent investor and built Meridian from nothing. On the night she dies, she is quietly unwinding her entire life: reconsidering a sale of the bar, reconsidering her decision to keep her past buried, and reconsidering whether to disappear again.

**Initial Mystery:** Who killed Elena Voss in the wine cellar, and why does everything point toward a robbery gone wrong?

**Central Mystery:** Why does the physical evidence in the wine cellar not actually match how Elena died — and why do the building's own security logs insist nobody could have been where the evidence says they were?

**True Solution (summary):** Elena was not killed in the wine cellar. She died on the rooftop deck after a raw, unplanned confrontation with Renata Cole — the hotel's building/security manager, Elena's former partner, and the one person who had spent years quietly protecting Elena's secrets without ever asking for anything in return. When Elena revealed she still intended to vanish and start over rather than stay, Renata tried to physically stop her from leaving; Elena pulled free, stumbled, and struck her head on a stone planter ledge, dying within minutes. In a panic, Renata used her intimate knowledge of the building's blind spots — the same skill she'd built running an unrelated after-hours side business — to move the body down the camera-free service stairwell to the wine cellar and stage a robbery. Her one mistake: she scrubbed the *main* camera system, but didn't know the service stairwell's keypad logged her personal access code on a separate, older subsystem.

## B. CAST

### SUS-01 — David Ostrow, 58
**Occupation:** Silent business partner / investor in Meridian (8 years).
**Relationship to victim:** Believed to be Elena's closest professional ally and friend.
**Personality:** Warm in public, controlling in private. Charismatic host energy that curdles into cornered irritability under pressure.
**Public image:** The beloved "uncle" of the bar — buys rounds, remembers names, seems to have no stake beyond friendship.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: He has serious gambling debts and needs a large cash injection soon.
- Secret #2: He has been secretly negotiating his *own* side arrangement with Titan Hospitality (the buyer) to guarantee his payout regardless of what Elena decides — without her knowledge.
- Secret #3: He has known for over a year that Elena was hiding a past identity, having stumbled onto it accidentally, and has said nothing — partly out of loyalty, partly because it gives him quiet leverage he's never had to use.

**Motive:** Elena's decision to kill the sale threatens to expose his side deal and leaves his debts unresolved.
**Opportunity:** Was in the building until at least 11:19 PM; knows the venue well; was alone with Elena shortly before her death.
**Alibi (claimed):** Left the building at 10:45 PM and went straight home.
**Actual whereabouts:** Confronted Elena on the rooftop deck around 11:05–11:12 PM about the collapsing deal; left angry, took a rideshare from the hotel entrance at 11:19 PM.
**Lie:** Claims he left at 10:45 PM.
**Reason for lying:** Doesn't want to be the last person seen arguing with Elena before her death — and doesn't want anyone asking *why* he was so desperate about the deal.

**Evidence that implicates him:** EVD-13 (badge re-entry log), EVD-14 (witness heard a shouting match on the roof).
**Evidence that clears him:** EVD-15 (rideshare pickup at 11:19 PM), EVD-16 (his 11:14 PM text to Whit confirming the deal was already dead — and that he'd left).
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I hadn't seen Elena in days" (Round 1) — directly contradicted once EVD-13/14 surface.
**Connection to another suspect:** Secret side arrangement with Whit Sokol (SUS-06).

### SUS-02 — Theo Marsh, 29
**Occupation:** Head bartender, Elena's protégé and presumed successor.
**Relationship to victim:** Mentee; treated almost like family, though never formally promised a stake in the business.
**Personality:** Intense, quietly ambitious, meticulous under pressure — the kind of person who over-explains when nervous.
**Public image:** The hardest worker in the building; universally liked by staff.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: He has been skimming roughly $400/week from the register to help cover his mother's medical bills.
- Secret #2: Titan Hospitality privately promised him a General Manager position if he helped smooth the ownership transition — meaning he had every reason to *want* the sale to happen, the opposite of an obvious "jealous heir" motive.
- Secret #3: He's been quietly recording snippets of conversations around the bar on his phone, telling himself it's for "service notes," but really because he's terrified of being blindsided by the sale and wants a paper trail to protect his job.

**Motive:** If Elena publicly backs out of the sale and someone eventually discovers his theft, he loses everything — his promised promotion and his job.
**Opportunity:** In the building all night; found the body; has keys to the cellar.
**Alibi (claimed):** Doing inventory in the kitchen continuously from 11:00–11:45 PM.
**Actual whereabouts:** Mostly true — except for a real six-minute gap around 11:12–11:18 PM when he stepped into the walk-in cooler to deal with a compressor alarm, not scanning inventory during that window.
**Lie:** Denies speaking to or seeing Elena after 10:45 PM.
**Reason for lying:** He *did* see her briefly around 10:50, when she snapped at him about the register discrepancy she'd just noticed — he doesn't want to admit that conversation happened, fearing it makes him look guilty of both theft and violence.

**Evidence that implicates him:** EVD-18 (inventory scan gap), EVD-20 (register shortfall).
**Evidence that clears him:** EVD-19 (cooler maintenance log independently places him in the kitchen during the gap).
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I barely talked to her today" — undercut once the register conversation surfaces.
**Connection to another suspect:** Secretly dating Iris Chen (SUS-05).

### SUS-03 — Priya Nandan, 35
**Occupation:** Hospitality-industry journalist; longtime Meridian regular.
**Relationship to victim:** Publicly a friendly acquaintance and occasional source-swap; privately, she is investigating Elena.
**Personality:** Sharp, controlled, good at getting people to talk without seeming to ask questions.
**Public image:** A respected, ethical journalist — well-liked, seen as an outsider to the bar's internal politics.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: She is secretly the daughter of Marcus Webb — the man who went to prison for the embezzlement scheme Elena orchestrated a decade ago under her old name.
- Secret #2: She's been quietly building a story on Titan Hospitality's acquisition tactics, using Meridian and Elena as an inside source without disclosing the personal connection.
- Secret #3: Months earlier, she confirmed Elena's true former identity through public records and has been deciding whether to expose her.

**Motive:** Deep personal grievance — her father lost years of his life for a crime he didn't fully commit, while Elena rebuilt hers under a new name.
**Opportunity:** Publicly left the event at 10:30 PM, but re-entered later through a side door.
**Alibi (claimed):** Left the tasting event at 10:30 PM and went home.
**Actual whereabouts:** Returned around 11:15 PM, confronted Elena on the rooftop deck, left again at approximately 11:26 PM.
**Lie:** Denies returning to the building after 10:30 PM.
**Reason for lying:** She got the confrontation — and the apology — she'd spent months working toward, and doesn't want her hidden identity or her return that night connected to a murder investigation before she's ready to tell that story on her own terms.

**Evidence that implicates her:** EVD-11 (napkin with her contact info in Elena's jacket, suggesting a private late exchange), EVD-12 (old records tying "Elena Kowalski" to the Marcus Webb case).
**Evidence that clears her:** EVD-10 (Elena's 11:27 PM voice memo, recorded alone, reflecting on the reconciliation — proving Elena was alive, alone, and at peace immediately after Priya's departure), EVD-26 (dock camera confirms her exit at 11:27 PM, before the death window).
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I haven't set foot in that building since I left the party" — directly false.
**Connection to another suspect:** Her presence and questions unknowingly overlap with Whit Sokol's movements on Floor 30.

### SUS-04 — Renata Cole, 46 — **THE CULPRIT**
**Occupation:** Building and security manager for the Aldwyn Hotel, overseeing Meridian's floors.
**Relationship to victim:** Former romantic partner, now Elena's closest confidante and quiet protector for years.
**Personality:** Guarded, precise, uncomfortable with vulnerability — the person everyone trusts with logistics and nobody thinks to ask about feelings.
**Public image:** Professional, a little distant, entirely reliable.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: She runs an unauthorized after-hours side business renting Meridian's private rooms to outside groups, skimming cash and covering it by editing the building's main security logs.
- Secret #2: She has known Elena's true former identity for years and has never told anyone — not out of leverage, but out of love.
- Secret #3: She and Elena had planned a private "last round" that night — a reconciliation drink on the roof — which Renata believed might be the start of Elena finally choosing to stay.

**Motive:** Not money, not exposure — heartbreak. When Elena told her, on the roof, that she still intended to disappear and start over rather than stay and face her past, Renata's years of quiet devotion collapsed into panic and desperation.
**Opportunity:** Full unsupervised access to every floor, every log, and the one interior stairwell with no working camera.
**Alibi (claimed):** Did building rounds until 10:20 PM, then remained in her 28th-floor office the rest of the night.
**Actual whereabouts:** Went to the rooftop deck at 11:28 PM for the planned drink with Elena; present at the time of Elena's death (~11:32 PM); moved the body to the wine cellar via the service stairwell between roughly 11:35–11:50 PM and staged the scene; returned to her office to edit the main camera log.
**Lie:** Claims she never returned to Floor 30 after 10:20 PM.
**Reason for lying (as she believes it, and as she frames it to herself):** She didn't mean for Elena to die — it was an accident born of a moment neither of them controlled — but knowing how it would look, and terrified of losing the last piece of Elena she had left (the bar, her memory, her secret), she chose to make it disappear the only way she knew how.

**Evidence that implicates her:** EVD-22 (service stairwell keypad log showing her personal code used twice that night), EVD-24 (her composure breaks specifically when the rooftop planter is mentioned).
**Evidence that "clears" her (until the deeper log is found):** EVD-21 (the main security system shows no roof access for her after 10:20 PM — a false clearance she engineered herself).
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I haven't been up to the roof since I locked it for the night" — technically about the *public* door, deliberately misleading about the service stairwell.
**Connection to another suspect:** Overheard fragments of David and Whit's private dealings weeks earlier, which deepened her sense that Elena's world — and her own place in it — was slipping away from her control.

### SUS-05 — Iris Chen, 27
**Occupation:** Server / bar-back at Meridian, three months on staff.
**Relationship to victim:** Elena's younger half-sister — a fact known only to the two of them.
**Personality:** Watchful, quick to deflect personal questions, warmer than she lets on.
**Public image:** A capable, quiet new hire; not seen as connected to Elena's inner circle at all.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: She is Elena's half-sister, who tracked her down after their mother's death and took the job at Elena's request to stay close without drawing attention.
- Secret #2: She's secretly dating Theo Marsh.
- Secret #3: A week earlier, she learned Elena was seriously planning to sell everything and leave the country under yet another new identity — abandoning Iris exactly as their family history had trained her to expect.

**Motive:** Raw hurt and anger at being abandoned a second time by the same person, just as they'd started rebuilding a relationship.
**Opportunity:** Working in the back of house all night with legitimate access to every service area, including the cellar and stairwell.
**Alibi (claimed):** Doing closing cleanup in the kitchen from 11:00–11:45 PM.
**Actual whereabouts:** Mostly true, but she slipped up to the rooftop deck briefly around 11:20 PM to confront Elena about the sale — before Priya arrived — and left after a short, heated but non-violent exchange.
**Lie:** Denies leaving the kitchen at all during that window.
**Reason for lying:** She's afraid that being placed at the confrontation, however brief and non-violent, will make her the obvious suspect — especially once anyone learns who she really is to Elena.

**Evidence that implicates her:** A staff sighting placing her near the service stairwell around 11:20 PM (folded into EVD-14/witness accounts); her hidden relationship to the victim once revealed looks concealment-heavy.
**Evidence that clears her:** Elena's 11:27 PM voice memo (EVD-10) makes no reference to any renewed conflict with Iris and describes a calm, resolved state of mind consistent with the earlier, already-cooled confrontation; timeline placement puts her exchange with Elena well before the fatal encounter with Renata.
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I never left the kitchen" — untrue, though the excursion itself turns out to be unrelated to the death.
**Connection to another suspect:** Secretly involved with Theo Marsh (SUS-02).

### SUS-06 — Whit Sokol, 51
**Occupation:** Acquisitions lead for Titan Hospitality Group, negotiating to buy Meridian.
**Relationship to victim:** Business counterpart; publicly introduced to staff only as "an old friend" to keep the deal quiet.
**Personality:** Smooth, deal-oriented, uncomfortable showing emotion in front of people he considers "the help."
**Public image:** An outsider nobody at the bar particularly likes or trusts.

**Hidden information:**
- Secret #1: Titan's acquisition contract contains a clause that voids the deal if Elena isn't personally involved through a transition period — meaning her death is a genuine financial disaster for him, not a windfall.
- Secret #2: He has been secretly negotiating a separate side arrangement with David Ostrow to guarantee David's payout independent of Elena, undercutting her position in the deal.
- Secret #3: Years earlier, he was investigated (never charged) after a different business owner died during a contentious Titan acquisition elsewhere — ruled accidental, but it still follows him.

**Motive:** Financial pressure to close the deal before a competing buyer surfaces — though her death actually works against that goal, which is precisely why he's a fair but ultimately misleading suspect.
**Opportunity:** Staying in the same hotel; was in the building until the early morning.
**Alibi (claimed):** Returned to his hotel room at 10:47 PM and stayed in for the night.
**Actual whereabouts:** Came back down to Floor 30 around 11:18 PM to try to salvage the contract after David's warning text, but retreated without going further than the bar area upon noticing Priya nearby.
**Lie:** Denies leaving his room after 10:47 PM.
**Reason for lying:** Doesn't want to be tied to a collapsing, contentious deal — or reminded of his past — the night his counterpart turns up dead.

**Evidence that implicates him:** His reputation and the prior investigated death (background, not physical evidence); the fact he clearly benefits from David's side deal.
**Evidence that clears him:** EVD-17 (hotel key-card log reinterpreted — the 10:47 PM tap only proves the room was reactivated, not that he stayed), cross-referenced with hallway staff placing him back on Floor 30 no later than 11:20 PM and never past the bar.
**Statement that becomes suspicious later:** "I was asleep by eleven" — undercut once the key-card quirk is understood.
**Connection to another suspect:** Secret side arrangement with David Ostrow (SUS-01).

## D. PLAYER DISCOVERY TIMELINE

**Phase 1 (Crime scene):** Body, staged robbery, forced-looking dock door, coroner's initial notes.
**Phase 2 (First interrogations):** Alibis established; small inconsistencies noted but nothing conclusive.
**Phase 3 (First breakthrough):** Lividity/forensic mismatch (EVD-05, EVD-06) proves the cellar is not the true death scene.
**Phase 4 (Second investigation):** Rooftop re-examined; bloodstain, earring, and the "R." calendar entry found.
**Phase 5 (Re-interrogation):** David's and Whit's contradictions exposed and explained; Theo's gap explained; Priya's return exposed.
**Phase 6 (The web):** Side-deal between David and Whit revealed; Iris and Theo's relationship revealed; Renata's side business revealed.
**Phase 7 (Major twist):** Priya's true identity as Marcus Webb's daughter revealed — the obvious "revenge" suspect, seemingly confirmed, immediately complicated by the 11:27 PM voice memo.
**Phase 8 (Final deduction):** Service stairwell keypad log (EVD-22) surfaces, contradicting Renata's "false clearance" from the main system.
**Phase 9 (Accusation):** Confrontation with Renata using the full chain of evidence.

## E. CRIME SCENE

**LOC-CELLAR (Wine Cellar, Floor 29) — Discovery Scene**
- Elena's body, positioned on her side near a broken shelf.
- Scattered bottles; a locked reserve case forced open, one bottle (1990 Bordeaux) missing.
- One earring missing from Elena's ear.
- Cellar concrete floor — dusty, undisturbed except around the body.
- No visible defensive wounds consistent with a struggle *in this room*.

**LOC-DOCK (Loading Dock, Floor 29)**
- Door found unlocked/ajar. Initially reads as forced entry; later explained as routinely propped for deliveries and never fully alarmed.

**LOC-ROOFTOP (Nightcap Deck, Floor 30) — True Scene (found in Phase 4)**
- A stone planter with a sharp lower edge.
- A faint, cleaned bloodstain revealed under luminol.
- A single earring stud wedged in the wood decking.
- An unfinished bottle of wine and two glasses, only one used.

**LOC-KITCHEN / SERVICE STAIRWELL (Floor 29–30)**
- POS terminal with Theo's scan history.
- Interior service stairwell — no working camera, keypad entry only, logs codes on a separate subsystem most staff don't know exists.

**LOC-OFFICE (Renata's Security Office, Floor 28)**
- Master log terminal (main system).
- A locked personal notebook (later found to contain the side-business ledger).

## G. SUSPECT INTERROGATIONS (representative dialogue beats)

*Each suspect has four rounds. Below are the key beats; full branching implementation should expand each into 3–5 selectable questions per round following this logic.*

### SUS-01 David Ostrow
- **R1:** Warm, deflecting. Claims he left at 10:45 "before things got quiet." Downplays his relationship with Elena to "just friends and business."
- **R2 (unlocked by EVD-13/14):** Confronted with the badge log and the witness account. Admits to the argument, insists he "just wanted her to reconsider," still lies about the *content* (doesn't mention Whit or his own debts).
- **R3 (unlocked by evidence of David/Whit connection, e.g. after Whit's R2):** Admits to the side deal with Whit under pressure; visibly ashamed, but firmly denies violence — offers his phone for the 11:14 PM text as proof he'd already left mentally, if not yet physically.
- **R4 (final):** Confronted with EVD-15/16, fully cleared of the murder; provides a clean secondary account of exactly what Elena said to him, which the player can cross-reference against Renata's later story.

### SUS-02 Theo Marsh
- **R1:** Guarded but cooperative. Insists he was doing inventory 11:00–11:45 PM, denies talking to Elena after 10:45.
- **R2 (unlocked by EVD-18):** Confronted with the scan gap. Panics, initially claims a bathroom break, then admits the register conversation with Elena happened around 10:50.
- **R3 (unlocked by EVD-20):** Confronted with the shortfall. Breaks down, admits the theft and the reason (his mother's medical bills), begs the player not to make it public.
- **R4 (unlocked by EVD-19):** Fully cleared of the murder once the cooler log corroborates his account; his recordings are revealed as self-protective, not sinister.

### SUS-03 Priya Nandan
- **R1:** Composed, sticks to "left at 10:30, went home." Deflects personal questions about her interest in Elena.
- **R2 (unlocked by EVD-11):** Confronted with the napkin. Admits to a "private conversation" earlier in the evening but still denies returning later.
- **R3 (unlocked by EVD-12):** Confronted with the identity records. Her composure finally breaks — reveals she is Marcus Webb's daughter and admits she came back that night to confront Elena.
- **R4 (unlocked by EVD-10/26):** Recounts the actual rooftop conversation — the apology, Elena's promise to clear her father's name — corroborated by the voice memo's tone and the dock camera's exit time. Fully cleared of violence.

### SUS-04 Renata Cole
- **R1:** Brisk, professional, almost too helpful with logistics. Claims rounds until 10:20 PM, then office all night.
- **R2 (unlocked by EVD-09):** Asked about "R." on Elena's calendar. Admits to the planned "nightcap" but claims Elena canceled and she "never went up."
- **R3 (unlocked by EVD-23):** Confronted with her side-business ledger. Admits the unauthorized rentals and the log editing, insisting it's unrelated to the murder — technically true about the *motive*, devastating for her credibility about *means*.
- **R4 (unlocked by EVD-22, cross-referenced with EVD-06/07/08 and EVD-24):** The player lays out the forensic mismatch, the earring, the bloodstain, the false main-system clearance, and the service stairwell code that only she could have entered — twice, four minutes and twenty-six minutes after the estimated time of death. Renata's composure finally breaks entirely; she confesses to the accidental fall and the cover-up.

### SUS-05 Iris Chen
- **R1:** Quiet, minimal, sticks to "kitchen all night," downplays her relationship to Elena and the bar generally.
- **R2 (unlocked by a staff sighting near the stairwell):** Admits to briefly stepping away, still doesn't say why.
- **R3 (unlocked by EVD-25):** Confronted with the texts to Theo; admits the relationship, and — once trust is established — admits she is Elena's half-sister and had gone up to confront her about the planned disappearance.

## J. RED HERRINGS

1. **The forced-looking dock door (EVD-04):** Suggests an external intruder. Real explanation: routinely left unlocked for deliveries; the killer used its plausibility, not its truth.
2. **David's shouting match and badge log (EVD-13/14):** Strongly suggests him as the last person with Elena. Real explanation: he left angry but alive, and is independently placed elsewhere before the death (EVD-15/16).
3. **Whit's criminal-adjacent reputation and hotel alibi (EVD-17):** Suggests a ruthless corporate killer. Real explanation: the deal needed Elena alive, and his actual movements never reached the roof.
4. **Theo's inventory gap and financial motive (EVD-18/20):** Suggests a desperate insider. Real explanation: an unrelated cooler alarm and an unrelated theft.
5. **Priya's hidden identity (EVD-12) surfacing right after her lie is caught (Round 3):** Deliberately timed to feel like a confession-in-progress. Real explanation: her business with Elena that night was reconciliation, not violence — confirmed by the voice memo.
6. **The "R." calendar initial:** Could plausibly refer to a background regular (referenced in early bar chatter as "Ray" or "Rosalind," a semi-regular patron) before context and later evidence narrow it to Renata.

## K. FALSE THEORIES

**Theory A — "David did it."** Motive (financial desperation), opportunity (last confirmed to argue with her), and a lie about his departure time all point his way. Collapses once his rideshare pickup and pre-death text are found — he simply wasn't there anymore.

**Theory B — "Whit had her killed to force the deal through."** Corporate motive, a suspicious past, and a shaky alibi. Collapses once the contract clause is understood (her death actively hurts the deal) and his movements are independently confirmed to stop at the bar.

**Theory C — "Theo snapped to protect his job and his theft."** Found the body, has real financial motive, lied about seeing her. Collapses once the cooler log independently accounts for his missing minutes.

**Theory D — "Priya finally got her revenge."** The strongest emotional theory once her identity is revealed — she has the deepest possible motive and admits to lying about returning. Collapses against the 11:27 PM voice memo and dock-exit timestamp, which place Elena alive, alone, and calm after Priya left — and which show Priya had already gotten what she came for.

## L. MAJOR TWISTS

1. **The cellar is not the crime scene.** Everything the player has been examining is a staged aftermath; the real event happened one floor up, in a different emotional register entirely.
2. **The journalist is the daughter of the man who took the fall for the victim's old crime.** The person who looks like the strongest suspect turns out to be the one who got closure without violence — and whose testimony ends up clearing, not condemning, her.
3. **The killer is the one person everyone trusted with logistics.** Not the money-driven partner, not the calculating buyer, not the desperate protégé — but the quiet, dependable building manager, whose devotion to Elena is exactly what made the death possible and exactly what made the cover-up so clean.

## M. FINAL DEDUCTION

The logical chain a player should be able to walk, in order:

1. The coroner's lividity report (EVD-05) proves Elena didn't die where she was found.
2. The stone residue in her wound (EVD-06) points to a location with a stone surface — inconsistent with the concrete cellar.
3. A second search of the rooftop (the only stone-heavy area in the venue) turns up a cleaned bloodstain and an earring (EVD-07/08), confirming it as the true scene.
4. The victim's calendar (EVD-09) shows a planned "Nightcap w/ R." at the relevant time.
5. David, Whit, Theo, Priya, and Iris are each placed on or near the roof at various points that night — but each is independently, evidentially accounted for *before* the estimated time of death (EVD-15/16, EVD-17+placement, EVD-19, EVD-10/26).
6. That leaves the death window (approx. 11:28–11:32 PM) unaccounted for by anyone — except the person the calendar entry names.
7. Renata's main-system log claims she was never on the roof after 10:20 PM (EVD-21) — but this is the *only* piece of evidence in the entire case that isn't corroborated by an independent source.
8. Requesting the separate, older service stairwell keypad log (EVD-22) reveals her personal code used twice — once right after the estimated time of death, once after enough time to move a body and stage a scene.
9. Her own ledger (EVD-23) explains exactly how she had the specific skill to edit one system and not think to check the other.
10. Confronted with all of it, her composure — steady through every other topic — finally breaks (EVD-24).

## N. TRUE SOLUTION

**Victim:** Elena Voss, born Elena Kowalski, spent a decade running from a financial crime she orchestrated and let someone else serve time for. On the last night of her life, she was trying — messily, imperfectly — to stop running: killing a sale that would have let her disappear with money, and preparing to make amends with the daughter of the man she'd wronged.

**Culprit:** Renata Cole.

**Motive:** Not greed, not exposure — grief in advance. After years of loving Elena quietly and protecting her secret without condition, Renata believed that night might finally be the moment Elena chose to stay. Instead, Elena told her she still intended to leave everything and everyone behind, including her.

**Method:** An unplanned physical struggle when Renata tried to stop Elena from walking away. Elena pulled free, stumbled backward, and struck her head on a stone planter ledge. Death was not instant, but no one else was present to help.

**Opportunity:** Renata's role as building/security manager gave her total access, deep knowledge of the venue's blind spots, and — crucially — a well-practiced, unrelated skill (editing security logs for her side business) that she reflexively applied to cover her tracks.

**Timeline:** Established in full in Section C.

**Crime scene:** Staged. The wine cellar "robbery" was assembled after the fact, from a real, unrelated valuable bottle and a real, unrelated broken shelf, specifically to misdirect toward an external intruder.

**Evidence:** Every item either supports the staged theory (Layer 1), undermines it (Layer 2), or narrows the true window to Renata alone (Layers 3–4), as detailed in Section F.

**Suspects:** Each lied to protect an unrelated secret — a side deal, a theft, a hidden identity, a hidden relationship, a damaged reputation — none of which was the murder.

**Red herrings:** Each was real evidence with an innocent, discoverable explanation, per Section J.

**False theories:** Each was reasonable given the evidence available at the time it was believed, per Section K.

**Final clue:** The service stairwell keypad log (EVD-22) — the one system Renata forgot existed, because it had never mattered to her side business before.
