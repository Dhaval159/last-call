# 2. CASE PREMISE

At approximately 11:05 PM, **Elias Voss**, a 42-year-old investigative journalist, is found dead inside his apartment.

At first glance, the death appears to be the result of a confrontation that went too far.

There are no obvious signs of forced entry.

Several people had reasons to be angry with Elias.

Four people become central to the investigation.

Each gives a different version of the evening.

One of them is responsible for Elias's death.

But the obvious suspect is not necessarily the culprit.

The investigation begins with one strange fact:

> Shortly before his death, Elias made a phone call that he apparently had no reason to make.

That call becomes the first thread in the case.

---

# 3. THE VICTIM

## Name

**Elias Voss**

## Age

42

## Occupation

Investigative journalist.

## Residence

Apartment 7B, Bellweather Heights.

## Personality

Elias was:

- Intelligent
- Persistent
- Observant
- Ambitious
- Difficult to intimidate
- Occasionally arrogant

He had built a reputation for investigating corruption and exposing people who believed they could hide behind money or influence.

He was not universally liked.

---

# 4. ELIAS'S CURRENT INVESTIGATION

Before his death, Elias had been investigating financial irregularities connected to a private development company.

The investigation was incomplete.

Elias believed someone connected to the investigation was attempting to stop him from publishing his findings.

He had not yet published the complete story.

Instead, he kept supporting material in his apartment.

This information becomes important later.

---

# 5. THE FOUR SUSPECTS

The four primary suspects are:

1. **Daniel Mercer**
2. **Maya Voss**
3. **Victor Hale**
4. **Nora Bennett**

Each has a legitimate reason to be considered suspicious.

Only one is the murderer.

---

# 6. SUSPECT 01 — DANIEL MERCER

## Age

39

## Occupation

Business partner and financial consultant.

## Relationship with Elias

Daniel previously worked with Elias on a financial investigation.

Their relationship deteriorated after Elias discovered financial discrepancies connected to Daniel's professional network.

## Personality

Daniel is:

- Calm
- Articulate
- Controlled
- Confident
- Difficult to read

He rarely becomes openly angry.

He prefers carefully chosen words.

---

# 7. DANIEL'S PUBLIC STORY

Daniel claims:

> He met Elias earlier in the evening but left the apartment before 10 PM.

According to Daniel:

- He and Elias argued.
- The argument was about Elias's investigation.
- Daniel left voluntarily.
- He returned home afterward.
- He did not contact Elias again.

Daniel presents himself as irritated but innocent.

---

# 8. DANIEL'S ACTUAL STORY

Daniel did return to Elias's apartment later.

He did not admit this.

He came back because he discovered that Elias possessed evidence that could expose Daniel's involvement in a financial fraud scheme.

Daniel wanted to retrieve the evidence.

He intended to pressure Elias into giving it to him.

The confrontation escalated.

Daniel struck Elias with a heavy object from the apartment.

Elias collapsed.

Daniel panicked.

He attempted to make the incident appear less deliberate.

He removed one important piece of evidence and left.

---

# 9. DANIEL'S MOTIVE

Daniel's motive is **self-preservation**.

If Elias published the investigation:

- Daniel's professional reputation would be destroyed.
- His financial activities could be exposed.
- He could face serious legal consequences.

Daniel believed he was protecting himself.

---

# 10. DANIEL'S CRITICAL LIE

Daniel says:

> He left the apartment before 10 PM and never returned.

This is false.

The central investigation eventually establishes that Daniel was present much later.

---

# 11. DANIEL'S ALIBI

Daniel claims he was at home after leaving Elias's apartment.

His alibi is intentionally weak.

He has no reliable independent witness confirming his exact location during the critical period.

---

# 12. DANIEL'S BEHAVIOR

During interrogation:

Early:

- Calm
- Cooperative
- Slightly dismissive

When asked about the argument:

- Defensive

When shown evidence proving he was inside the apartment later:

- Attempts to explain it away

When confronted with the strongest contradiction:

- Becomes noticeably less controlled

When the player presents the final evidence:

- Stops trying to maintain his original story.

## Age

37

## Occupation

Elias's younger sister.

## Personality

Maya is:

- Intelligent
- Emotional
- Protective
- Direct
- Easily frustrated by accusations

---

# 14. MAYA'S PUBLIC STORY

Maya admits she argued with Elias earlier that evening.

She says:

- Elias had become obsessed with his investigation.
- She wanted him to stop putting himself in danger.
- She left after their argument.
- She did not return.

Maya appears suspicious because she had a serious emotional argument with the victim.

---

# 15. MAYA'S SECRET

Maya had recently discovered that Elias was planning to expose someone connected to her workplace.

She was afraid the investigation might affect her career.

She therefore asked Elias to stop.

She does not initially reveal this.

Her secrecy makes her look more suspicious than she actually is.

---

# 16. MAYA'S ALIBI

Maya left before the critical period.

A transportation record and another independently verifiable detail establish that she was elsewhere later.

Maya is therefore not the murderer.

---

# 17. MAYA'S ROLE IN THE MYSTERY

Maya functions as a **false lead**.

She has:

- A motive to argue with Elias.
- A secret.
- Emotional involvement.
- A reason to hide information.

But she did not kill him.

Her purpose is to teach the player:

> Hiding something does not automatically mean someone is the murderer.

# 21. VICTOR'S ALIBI

# 22. VICTOR'S ROLE

> "The journalist was investigating Victor, so Victor must have killed him."

This theory should eventually collapse.

# 29. IMPORTANT TIMELINE DISTINCTION

The player initially receives an approximate incident window.

The exact timeline becomes clearer through evidence.

The central timeline mystery is:

> **How could Daniel have committed the crime if he claims he left before 10 PM?**

The answer is:

> He returned.

# 30. THE LAST CALL

The mysterious 10:42 PM call is one of the case's central clues.

The call is not itself the murder.

It serves two purposes.

### First

It establishes that Elias was alive and actively communicating shortly before the critical event.

### Second

It provides a path toward identifying the sequence of events that occurred afterward.

The exact recipient and information contained in the call are defined in the evidence documentation.

# 31. THE MURDER

## Culprit

**Daniel Mercer**

## Motive

Prevent Elias from exposing Daniel's financial misconduct.

## Method

Daniel strikes Elias with a heavy object inside the apartment.

## Opportunity

Daniel returns to the apartment after initially leaving.

## Cover-up

Daniel lies about returning and removes important investigative evidence.

---

# 32. WHY DANIEL IS NOT IMMEDIATELY OBVIOUS

Daniel is deliberately designed to be believable.

His initial story contains enough truth to appear credible.

He genuinely:

- Met Elias.
- Argued with him.
- Left the apartment.

The lie is that he **never returned**.

This makes the investigation depend on timeline reconstruction rather than a simple motive check.

---

# 33. THE CENTRAL MYSTERY QUESTION

The case revolves around:

> **If Daniel left before 10 PM, who was with Elias after the last call?**

The player gradually discovers:

1. Elias was alive later than initially believed.
2. Someone returned to the apartment.
3. Daniel's story contains a critical gap.
4. Physical evidence places Daniel inside later.
5. Daniel had a reason to remove evidence.
6. His timeline ultimately becomes impossible to defend.

# 48. FINAL CASE STATEMENT

**The Last Call** is a murder mystery built around a simple but powerful idea:

> A person can tell the truth about most of an evening and still lie about the one moment that matters.

Daniel really did meet Elias.

Daniel really did argue with him.

Daniel really did leave.

But Daniel also returned.

That missing piece of the timeline is what ultimately breaks the case open.

The player must discover it.

**That is the mystery.**
