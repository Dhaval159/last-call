# THE LAST CALL
# TIMELINE BIBLE

**Case ID:** CASE-001  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical Timeline Specification  
**Purpose:** Defines the complete chronology of Case 001, including the true timeline, the player's initial understanding, suspect statements, contradictions, and final reconstruction.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document is the authoritative source for all chronological events in **The Last Call**.

The implementation AI must use this document when determining:

- When events occurred
- Where suspects were
- What each person knew
- What the player knows at each stage
- Which statements are truthful
- Which statements are lies
- When evidence becomes relevant
- How the final reconstruction works

The implementation AI must not invent alternate critical timelines.

---

# 2. DATE

The exact calendar date is intentionally not important to the mystery.

The case takes place during a single evening and night.

The game should display the timeline using:

**PM / AM clock time**

rather than requiring a specific calendar date.

---

# 3. CRITICAL TIME RANGE

The most important period is:

**9:00 PM – 11:00 PM**

The key events occur between:

**9:05 PM and approximately 10:55 PM.**

The murder occurs after Elias's 10:42 PM outgoing call.

---

# 4. COMPLETE TRUE TIMELINE

The following is the canonical truth.

```text
EARLIER THAT DAY
        ↓

        ↓
Nora works with Elias on documents
        ↓
Nora secretly keeps copies of some files
        ↓
Maya becomes worried about Elias
        ↓
Victor continues conflict with Elias
        ↓
Daniel realizes Elias is close to exposing the financial misconduct
        ↓
EVENING
```

# 5. 8:00 PM — NORA'S WORK

Nora is working with Elias on investigation material.

She helps organize:

- Financial records
- Company transactions
- Research notes
- Supporting documents

Elias is becoming increasingly confident that he has discovered something important.

Nora realizes the investigation could become dangerous.

She secretly keeps copies of some documents.

This becomes her secret.

---

# 6. 8:30 PM — MAYA ARRIVES

Maya visits Elias.

She is concerned about his investigation.

She believes he is taking unnecessary risks.

---

# 7. 8:40 PM — MAYA AND ELIAS ARGUE

Maya tells Elias:

> "You need to stop."

Elias refuses.

The argument becomes emotional.

Maya leaves angry.

This is the basis of her suspicious appearance.

---

# 8. 9:05 PM — MAYA LEAVES

Maya leaves the area.

Her transportation record later supports this.

She does not return.

This is important because Maya's argument is emotionally suspicious, but her timeline does not overlap with the murder.

---

# 9. 9:15 PM — ELIAS CONTINUES WORKING

Elias returns to his investigation.

He reviews financial documents.

He realizes that an important connection involving Daniel is becoming clearer.

He continues working alone.

---

# 10. 9:25 PM — VICTOR'S DINNER

Victor attends his private dinner.

The reservation and payment records establish his presence.

He remains away from Elias's apartment during the critical period.

---

# 11. 9:30 PM — NORA AT HOME

Nora is at home.

Her digital activity later supports this.

She is not physically present at the apartment.

---

# 12. 9:40 PM — DANIEL'S FIRST VISIT

Daniel arrives at Elias's apartment.

Elias allows him inside.

There is no forced entry.

This explains E009.

---

# 13. 9:45 PM — DANIEL AND ELIAS ARGUE

Daniel and Elias discuss the financial investigation.

Elias accuses Daniel of knowing more than he admits.

Daniel denies wrongdoing.

The argument becomes serious.

---

# 14. 9:50 PM — DANIEL LEAVES

Daniel leaves the apartment.

This part of his statement is true.

This is important.

Daniel's lie is not that he was never there.

His lie is that he **never returned**.

---

# 15. 9:55 PM — ELIAS CONTINUES INVESTIGATION

Elias returns to his desk.

He reviews the investigation material.

He realizes that the evidence could expose Daniel.

An important file is still in his possession.

---

# 16. 10:05 PM — DANIEL REALIZES THE RISK

Daniel becomes increasingly concerned about what Elias has discovered.

He knows that the investigation could seriously damage him.

He decides to return.

This is the turning point.

---

# 17. 10:20 PM — DANIEL RETURNS

Daniel returns to the apartment.

Elias lets him inside because Daniel was already known to him.

There is still no forced entry.

This explains E009.

---

# 18. 10:25 PM — SECOND CONFRONTATION

Daniel demands that Elias hand over the investigation material.

Elias refuses.

The argument escalates.

---

# 19. 10:30 PM — FINANCIAL FILE IS THREATENED

Elias makes it clear that he intends to continue the investigation.

Daniel realizes that talking will not solve the problem.

He becomes desperate.

---

# 20. 10:35 PM — PHYSICAL CONFRONTATION

A physical confrontation occurs.

A glass is knocked over and breaks.

This explains E007.

---

# 21. 10:37 PM — PAPERWEIGHT IS USED

Daniel strikes Elias with the heavy paperweight.

Elias is fatally injured.

The paperweight is left near the desk.

This explains E008.

---

# 22. 10:40 PM — DANIEL PANICS

Daniel realizes what he has done.

He looks for the investigation material that could expose him.

He removes an important financial file.

This explains E005.

---

# 23. 10:42 PM — LAST OUTGOING CALL

The canonical timeline requires Elias's phone record to show an outgoing call at:

**10:42 PM**

This establishes that Elias was alive at that time.

The call is the final major chronological anchor.

This explains E002.

---

# 24. IMPORTANT TIMELINE NOTE

The 10:42 PM call does **not** mean Daniel left before the call.

It establishes that Elias was alive during the confrontation period.

The game must not incorrectly imply that the call automatically identifies the recipient as the murderer.

The call is primarily a time anchor.

---

# 25. 10:45 PM — DANIEL LEAVES

Daniel leaves the apartment.

He takes the important financial material with him.

He attempts to make the scene look less connected to him.

He does not successfully erase all evidence.

---

# 26. 10:50 PM — DANIEL BEGINS FALSE ALIBI

Daniel returns home.

He later claims that he remained there after leaving Elias.

This becomes his false alibi.

---

# 27. APPROXIMATELY 10:55 PM — CRITICAL PERIOD ENDS

The investigation treats this as the end of the primary critical window.

The exact minute of Elias's death is intentionally not stated to the player.

The player must work with the evidence to establish the sequence.

### Claims

### Truth

Correct.

---

## VICTOR

### Claims

At private dinner.

### Truth

Correct.

---

## NORA

### Claims

Left before evening and stayed home.

### Truth

Correct regarding physical presence.

Her hidden secret concerns copied files, not her location.

---

## DANIEL

### Claims

Left before 10 PM and never returned.

### Truth

Left around 9:50 PM.

Returned around 10:20 PM.

Left again around 10:45 PM.

His second visit is the critical lie.

# 33. TIMELINE CONTRADICTION

The key contradiction is:

```text
DANIEL CLAIM:
"I left before 10 PM and never returned."

        VS

TRUE EVIDENCE:
Daniel was present again after 10 PM.
```

This contradiction is the backbone of the final deduction.

# 34. WHY THE TIMELINE MATTERS

The timeline should teach the player that:

**Motive alone is insufficient.**

Victor has motive.

Maya has conflict with Elias.

Nora has secrets.

Daniel has motive too.

But only Daniel's timeline cannot survive the evidence.

# 38. NO IMPOSSIBLE TIMELINE

The implementation must prevent:

- Two suspects being in two distant locations simultaneously.
- Maya returning after E017 clears her.
- Victor appearing at the apartment during the critical period.
- Nora appearing physically at the apartment during the critical period.
- Daniel being absent from the apartment after E018.

---

# 39. TIMELINE DEDUCTION PUZZLE

The main timeline puzzle should ask the player to determine:

### Question 1

Who was definitely away during the critical period?

Answer:

- Maya
- Victor
- Nora

### Question 2

Who had a weak/unverified alibi?

Answer:

- Daniel

### Question 3

Who was physically present after claiming to have left?

Answer:

- Daniel

### Question 4

Who had motive connected to the missing financial material?

Answer:

- Daniel

# 41. IMPORTANT ORDERING NOTE

Do not require the player to know the exact 10:37 PM attack time before solving the case.

The exact minute is part of the canonical truth.

The player primarily needs to establish:

```text
Daniel returned
        ↓
Daniel was present during the critical period
        ↓
Elias was alive at 10:42
        ↓
Daniel had motive
        ↓

```

# 44. IMPLEMENTATION RULE

The timeline must be deterministic.

Do not generate random times.

Do not move events randomly.

Do not generate different suspect locations.

Every playthrough of Case 001 uses the same canonical chronology.

The challenge comes from discovering the truth, not from randomizing the truth.

---

# 45. FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The timeline should make the player experience this realization:

> "Everyone had a story."

Then:

> "Three stories fit the evidence."

And finally:

> "Daniel's doesn't."

That is the purpose of the timeline system.
