# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the four primary suspects in **The Last Call**.

It is the authoritative source for:

- Character identities
- Personalities
- Relationships
- Public stories
- Hidden truths
- Motives
- Alibis
- Secrets
- Interview behavior
- Evidence reactions
- Character arcs

The implementation AI must not invent a new culprit, change a suspect's innocence/guilt, or create major backstory that contradicts this document.

# 3. SUSPECT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Every suspect must feel capable of being guilty.

Each suspect must have:

- Something to hide
- A reason to lie
- A connection to Elias
- At least one suspicious behavior
- A believable explanation for that behavior

However, their secrets must have different meanings.

The central lesson is:

> **People can lie without being murderers.**

---

# 4. SUS-01 — DANIEL MERCER

## Identity

**Full Name:** Daniel Mercer  
**Age:** 39  
**Occupation:** Financial consultant  
**Relationship to Elias:** Former professional associate  
**Status:** **CULPRIT**

---

## Personality

Daniel is:

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Articulate
- Controlled
- Persuasive
- Image-conscious
- Patient
- Comfortable with confrontation
- Skilled at redirecting questions

He rarely loses his temper immediately.

His primary defense mechanism is control.

He tries to make the investigator feel as though the question itself is unreasonable.

---

## Appearance Direction

Daniel should visually communicate:

- Professionalism
- Wealth without extravagance
- Controlled body language
- A carefully maintained appearance

He should look like someone who could comfortably walk into a business meeting.

He should not visually look like an obvious villain.

---

## Relationship With Elias

Daniel previously worked with Elias on financial investigations.

Initially, Elias trusted Daniel's expertise.

That relationship deteriorated after Elias began discovering financial irregularities connected to Daniel's professional network.

Daniel realized that Elias was getting too close.

---

# 5. DANIEL'S PUBLIC STORY

Daniel admits:

- He met Elias that evening.
- They argued.
- The argument concerned Elias's investigation.
- He left before 10 PM.
- He went home.
- He did not return.
- He had no further contact with Elias.

Everything except the last part of the timeline is deliberately close to the truth.

---

# 6. DANIEL'S HIDDEN TRUTH

Daniel did return.

He returned because Elias had discovered information that could expose Daniel's financial misconduct.

Daniel wanted to retrieve the relevant investigation material.

He initially intended to pressure Elias.

The confrontation escalated.

Daniel struck Elias with the heavy paperweight.

Daniel panicked.

He removed an important financial file.

He then left and constructed a false timeline.

---

# 7. DANIEL'S MOTIVE

Daniel's motive is self-preservation.

Elias's investigation threatened:

- Daniel's professional reputation
- His financial interests
- His relationships with business associates
- His freedom from potential legal consequences

Daniel believed destroying the investigation was the safest way to protect himself.

---

# 8. DANIEL'S SECRET

Daniel knows that Elias possessed evidence connecting him to financial misconduct.

Daniel is aware that Elias was close to publishing the investigation.

Daniel therefore has a much stronger reason to fear Elias than he admits.

---

# 9. DANIEL'S ALIBI

Daniel claims:

> He was home after leaving Elias's apartment.

This is deliberately weak.

There is no reliable independent evidence establishing his exact location throughout the critical period.

The alibi exists mainly as a verbal claim.

---

# 10. DANIEL'S CORE LIE

The critical lie is:

> **"I never came back."**

The investigation must eventually prove that this statement is false.

This is more important than proving that Daniel was at the apartment earlier, because Daniel already admits the earlier visit.

---

# 11. DANIEL — INTERVIEW BEHAVIOR

## First Interview

Daniel is cooperative.

He gives concise answers.

He acts as though the investigation is wasting everyone's time.

Example tone:

> "I already told you what happened. We argued. I left."

---

## When Asked About Elias

Daniel describes Elias as:

- Obsessive
- Difficult
- Reckless
- Too convinced that he was right

Daniel avoids sounding emotionally attached.

---

## When Asked About the Argument

Daniel admits the argument was serious.

He says Elias accused him of being involved in questionable financial activity.

Daniel denies wrongdoing.

---

## When Asked About His Departure

Daniel becomes precise.

He insists that he left before 10 PM.

This precision is intentional.

---

## When Asked If He Returned

Daniel gives a direct denial.

He should not accidentally confess.

---

## When Shown Weak Evidence

Daniel remains calm.

He explains why the evidence proves nothing.

---

## When Shown E006 — Desk Fingerprint

Daniel says he had already been inside earlier.

Therefore the fingerprint does not prove that he returned.

This is a valid defense.

---

## When Shown E002 — Last Call

Daniel argues that the call does not establish who was present afterward.

Again, this is a valid defense.

---

## When Shown E018 — Return Evidence

Daniel becomes noticeably less comfortable.

He attempts to explain the evidence as an error or misunderstanding.

---

## When Shown E019 — Financial Investigation File

Daniel becomes defensive.

He tries to minimize his connection to the investigation.

---

## Final Contradiction

When the player connects:

- His statement
- Elias's 10:42 PM call
- His later presence
- His motive
- His physical connection to the apartment

Daniel can no longer maintain the original story.

He should stop arguing and become quiet.

---

# 14. DANIEL — INTERVIEW RULES

The AI must not make Daniel confess too early.

Daniel should never say:

- "I went back."

# 15. SUS-02 — MAYA VOSS

## Identity

**Full Name:** Maya Voss  
**Age:** 37  
**Occupation:** Architect  
**Relationship to Elias:** Younger sister  
**Status:** **INNOCENT**

---

# 16. MAYA'S PERSONALITY

Maya is:

- Intelligent
- Emotional
- Protective
- Direct
- Impatient
- Honest when calm
- Defensive when she feels Elias's memory is being disrespected

She is much more emotionally expressive than Daniel.

---

# 17. MAYA'S RELATIONSHIP WITH ELIAS

Elias and Maya were siblings who cared about each other but frequently disagreed.

Maya believed Elias took unnecessary risks.

Elias believed Maya did not understand the importance of his work.

Their relationship had become strained.

---

# 18. MAYA'S PUBLIC STORY

Maya admits:

- She visited Elias.
- They argued.
- She wanted him to stop the investigation.
- She left shortly after 9 PM.
- She did not return.

This is substantially true.

---

# 19. MAYA'S SECRET

Maya was worried that Elias's investigation could affect people connected to her professional life.

She therefore asked him to stop.

She initially hides this because she fears appearing selfish.

---

# 20. MAYA'S MOTIVE APPEARANCE

From the player's perspective, Maya appears suspicious because:

- She had a serious argument with Elias.
- She wanted him to stop.
- She hides information.
- She is emotionally upset about his death.

However, none of this makes her the murderer.

---

# 21. MAYA'S ALIBI

Maya left the area shortly after the argument.

E017 supports her timeline.

She cannot have been present during the critical later period.

---

# 22. MAYA — INTERVIEW BEHAVIOR

## First Interview

Maya is upset.

She may initially sound defensive.

She says she and Elias argued.

---

## When Asked Why They Argued

She says:

> "He was putting himself in danger."

She does not immediately explain the professional connection.

---

## When Asked Whether She Hated Elias

She rejects the suggestion.

She explains that being angry with someone does not mean wanting them dead.

---

## When Asked About Her Departure

She gives an approximate time.

She is not initially certain of every minute.

This makes her statement look weaker than it actually is.

---

## When Shown E017

Her timeline becomes much more credible.

---

## When Shown E019

Maya becomes concerned about what Elias had discovered.

She admits that she knew the investigation could affect her.

---

**Age:** 51  
**Occupation:** Property developer  
**Relationship to Elias:** Subject of investigation  
**Status:** **INNOCENT**

---

# 27. VICTOR'S PERSONALITY

Victor is:

- Confident
- Wealthy
- Polished
- Authoritative
- Impatient
- Used to controlling conversations
- Comfortable with legal threats

He is the most intimidating suspect.

---

# 28. VICTOR'S RELATIONSHIP WITH ELIAS

Elias was investigating Victor's company.

Victor considered Elias a nuisance.

Their relationship was openly hostile.

Victor had threatened legal action against Elias.

---

# 29. VICTOR'S PUBLIC STORY

Victor admits:

- Elias was investigating his company.
- They had conflicts.
- He was angry with Elias.
- He did not visit the apartment that night.
- He attended a private dinner.

---

# 30. VICTOR'S SECRET

Victor had threatened Elias before.

He does not initially disclose how serious the confrontation became.

He is afraid that admitting the threat will make him appear guilty.

---

# 31. VICTOR'S MOTIVE APPEARANCE

Victor has the strongest obvious motive.

The player should reasonably suspect him early.

However, the investigation must eventually establish that motive alone is insufficient.

---

# 32. VICTOR'S ALIBI

Victor's dinner records place him elsewhere during the critical period.

E015 supports the alibi.

---

# 33. VICTOR — INTERVIEW BEHAVIOR

## First Interview

Victor is irritated.

He makes it clear that he dislikes Elias.

---

## When Asked Whether He Wanted Elias Dead

He strongly denies it.

He says Elias was a professional problem, not a personal obsession.

---

## When Asked About Threats

He initially minimizes them.

Eventually he admits he threatened legal action.

---

## When Shown E015

Victor becomes more confident.

His timeline is supported.

---

## When Shown Daniel's Evidence

Victor becomes interested.

He may point out that Daniel was closer to Elias than he initially admitted.

Victor should not magically know the entire solution.

# 47. SUSPECT INTERVIEW DESIGN

The player should not simply click through one fixed conversation.

Interviews should contain:

- Initial questions
- Follow-up questions
- Evidence presentation
- Contradiction opportunities
- Optional questions
- Character reactions

However, the underlying facts must remain fixed.

---

# 48. QUESTION TYPES

## Background

Used to understand the suspect.

## Timeline

Used to establish movement.

## Relationship

Used to establish motive.

## Secret

Used to reveal hidden information.

## Evidence

Used to test statements.

## Contradiction

Used to expose lies.

---

# 49. SUSPECT RESPONSE RULES

A suspect should not always say:

> "That's impossible."

Responses should vary naturally.

Possible reactions:

- Agreement
- Denial
- Deflection
- Explanation
- Confusion
- Anger
- Silence
- Admission of a minor secret

---

# 50. NO RANDOM CHARACTER FACTS

The implementation AI must not invent:

- New family members
- New jobs
- New crimes
- New relationships
- New motives
- New locations
- New witnesses
- New romantic relationships
- New secret accomplices

unless a future version explicitly expands the case bible.

---

# 51. NO SECRET SECOND CULPRIT

There is exactly one murderer:

**Daniel Mercer.**

No suspect is secretly helping Daniel.

No hidden mastermind exists.

No twist should reveal another killer.

---

# 52. CHARACTER KNOWLEDGE RULES

Characters only know what they logically could know.

### Daniel knows

- His own actions
- His motive
- His return
- What he removed
- His interactions with Elias

### Maya knows

- Her argument with Elias
- Her concerns
- Limited information about the investigation

### Victor knows

- His conflict with Elias
- His own company
- His threats
- His dinner timeline

### Nora knows

- Elias's research
- Documents she handled
- Her copied files
- Some investigation details

No innocent suspect should possess impossible knowledge of Daniel's actions.

---

# 53. PLAYER PERCEPTION GOALS

Early game:

**Victor feels most suspicious.**

Middle game:

**Maya and Nora become plausible alternatives.**

Late game:

**Daniel's timeline becomes increasingly suspicious.**

Final:

**Daniel becomes the only suspect whose story cannot survive the evidence.**

---

# 54. FINAL CHARACTER DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The suspects are not meant to be:

> Good person, good person, good person, evil person.

They are:

> Four people with different reasons to hide the truth.

Only one person's hidden truth is murder.

That distinction should drive the entire interrogation experience.
